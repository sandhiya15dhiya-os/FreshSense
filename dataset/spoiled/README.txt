SPOILED — Put your WHITE film photos here
=============================================

What to photograph:
- The smart film patch when it is in its SPOILED state
- White / colorless film counts as "Spoiled"

How many:
- At least 15-20 photos for good accuracy

Tips for better accuracy:
- Take photos in different lighting (daylight, room light, phone flash)
- Vary the angle and distance slightly between photos
- Make sure the film patch is clearly visible/centered in the frame
- Avoid blurry photos

Accepted file types: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp

After adding photos, delete this file (or leave it, it will be ignored)
and run:
    python train_model.py
